Choose a team

Pick a team on the left to customize its kit, players, model, fans, celebrations, sounds and sponsors. Matches between teams are played from the Matches page.

Help

Keyboard. Tab and Shift Tab move between controls. Enter or Space activates a control. Arrow keys, Home and End move through menus and lists. Esc closes panels and returns focus. Focus the picture for camera and playback keys. Field arrows turn the camera, Watch uses Alt and arrows. Plus and minus zoom. Color fields also accept hex values.

Getting started

Watch, open a file or choose a source. Recordings, browse saved games. Record a batch with cuju.render.Recorder. Watch live with cuju.render.watch. Matches, choose teams and request a game. Play, choose a side and start.

Pages

Home, choose an activity. Teams, import submissions and edit players. Field, arrange the pitch and stadium. Help, find controls and guides. Ctrl K, or the search button in the top bar, opens the command palette on any page.

Making a team

A team is a submission folder in the format of Kaggle's football competition, whose main.py defines the agent as its last function. Add the folder on the Teams page, or with cuju render team add, then pick the team on Matches or Play. The built-in AI is always a team.

Play controls

Arrow keys move. S passes short, W passes long, A passes high or tackles, D shoots. Hold E to sprint and C to dribble. Esc pauses. The input settings on Play change these keys and the dead zone of the stick. On a touch screen a stick and the buttons of play show over the game.

Controller, L left stick or D pad moves. A passes short, B shoots, X passes high or tackles, Y passes long. RT sprints, RB dribbles. pauses, shows controls. The controls card in Play sets up other controllers.

Home pauses your game. Return to Play to Resume or Quit. Quit to Home ends the game and saves it when Save to Recordings is selected.
